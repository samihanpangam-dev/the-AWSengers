Privacy

Infinity Tool Box is designed to run in your browser. Inputs and files stay on your device unless a tool explicitly sends them to a service you configure, such as the optional AWSengers backend.

Local storage

The homepage stores only your theme preference. The AWSengers page stores only its configured API URL. Clear these values from your browser settings at any time.

Third-party services

When you use the AWSengers agent, files and prompts are sent to the API URL you provide. Review that service's privacy policy before uploading sensitive data. Do not upload credentials, personal health records, payment data, or confidential documents unless you control and trust the configured endpoint.

Tools provide informational results and are not a substitute for professional medical, legal, tax, or financial advice.
